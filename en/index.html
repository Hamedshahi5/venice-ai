Privacy modes

Venice shows a privacy mode on every model: Anonymized (your identity is hidden from the model provider, but the provider may process your prompt), Private (the default: zero-data-retention infrastructure, nothing stored), and TEE and E2EE (Pro: hardware-secured enclaves; with E2EE your prompt is encrypted on your device and only decrypted inside the enclave, but web search and memory are disabled).

Plans & cost

Free: 10 text and 15 image prompts per day. Pro (listed at about $18/month): unlimited text, up to 1,000 image prompts a day, 100 monthly credits for premium features such as video, music and frontier models, plus options like removing image watermarks and the mature filter. Pro+ and Max add far more credits (7,500 and 22,500 per month). The API is billed separately. Check venice.ai/pricing for current prices.

Good to know

Subscriptions are generally non-refundable (cancel before renewal). Premium features consume credits. Private mode relies on commitments from Venice and its partners, and inference still runs on remote servers; use E2EE for the most sensitive work. Chat history lives in your browser, so clearing browser data deletes it unless you back it up. “Uncensored” means fewer restrictions, not no responsibility: follow the law and the terms of service.
